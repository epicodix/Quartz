> **arXiv**: [2601.16725](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16725) | ⬆️ 171 → [[DD-002|Deep Dive 보기]]
> **태그**: `longcat-flash-thinking` `mixture-of-experts` `agentic-ai` `reinforcement-learning` `heavy-thinking` `test-time-scaling` `llm-reasoning`
